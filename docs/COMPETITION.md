## The problem: keeping an emerging community organized through recognition delays
**Headline:** OSLE's published application post describes a narrow September 1-14 Fall application window; the
founder's two November 4 decisions explicitly name **Fall 2027**, not Spring 2027, as their next application cycle.

Keep these three kinds of claim separate:
1. **Hofstra's documented process** (current source first):
   - The June 2025 post says new-club applications open **Sept 1–14** for Fall ("Applications open on GIHU on the first day of classes each
     semester"). They require **a constitution, a full e-board of President, Vice President, Secretary and Treasurer, and a
     roster of 15 additional students** (so 19 people), then a presentation to a **Recognition Committee** (two e-board
     members must attend). Denied groups can appeal "within 10 days of the decision".
     — [OSLE, "Fall New Club Application", GetInvolvedHU (posted June 13, 2025)](https://hofstra.campuslabs.com/engage/news/323408)
   - The organization handbook adds: a **full-time Hofstra faculty/staff advisor** is required; the New Club Registration
     Committee is "chaired by the Associate Director of Student Leadership and Engagement with representatives from OSLE,
     Recreation and Intramural Sports, Event Management and the Student Government Association"; and until a group is
     recognized, its "activities … will be limited to meetings and membership recruitment ONLY."
     — [Undergraduate Student Organizations Handbook (PDF, dated Sept 2016)](https://www.hofstra.edu/pdf/studentaffairs/studentactivities/osle-handbook-undergraduate-student-organizations.pdf)

   live; members explicitly opt in to be counted for recognition; dashboard shows "X/15 supporters" and drafts the request
   email with top supporters CC'd. *(verified: e2e checks "recognition counts members who said yes", "a moderator cannot

   *(verified: "the VP checks the member in by pass", "officer sees RSVP → check-in rate")*

   *(verified: "muted members get no reminders"; live scheduler delivered a reminder)*

   personalized recommendations and within-club leaderboards also exist. *(historical test: "Discover says clubs are listed A–Z")*
5. **Privacy by design:** campus-only sign-up, ratings officers can't trace to a person, contact info only if shared,
   explicit consent for recognition contact, disclaimer of Hofstra non-affiliation inside the app. *(verified: ratings
   unreadable, non-Hofstra email refused, server-only helpers blocked)*
6. **Shared links that convert:** a club link opens a public page and turns into "Join" or "Help build this group".
